# Philosophy

The design decisions that make Jobsmith what it is. Contributors: these are load-bearing. A change that violates one of these is a different product.

## You approve everything

Jobsmith never applies, never sends, never posts. It drafts; the human reviews and sends. This is a quality position, a safety position, and a trust position all at once. Auto-apply volume is a losing strategy that damages candidates' standing with recruiters, and an agent that acts on your behalf without review will eventually embarrass you at the worst possible moment.

## Honesty is a feature

The resume that gets you the interview has to survive the interview. Jobsmith works from verified facts only: what your materials say and what you told it directly. It will not inflate. In the other direction, it actively digs for the impact you failed to claim, because experienced professionals undersell far more often than they oversell. Both corrections are its job.

## Quality over volume

Fewer, better applications. Warm paths over cold blasts. A tailored kit for a role worth having beats twenty generic submissions. The pipeline's approval gate exists so effort goes only where you decided it should.

## Direct manipulation

You manage your pipeline by clicking a table or editing a spreadsheet, never by narrating status changes to an AI. Jobsmith reads your tracker and catches up at the start of each session. Chat is for work, not bookkeeping.

## Graceful degradation, never gates

The recommended stack (Claude Code plus Notion) gives the smoothest experience, and everything still works without it: no account, no API key, and no specific model is ever required. Every capability has a degraded path. Missing pieces produce suggestions, not walls.

## Any industry, really

Job tooling defaults to tech. Jobsmith treats your field's actual hiring channels as a research problem it solves for you during onboarding. A nurse's channel plan and an attorney's channel plan should look nothing alike, and neither should look like a software engineer's.

## Built for stolen minutes

Job searching happens in the gaps of a full life, often quietly alongside a current job. Every flow is interruptible and resumable. Nothing assumes a free afternoon, and nothing nags.
